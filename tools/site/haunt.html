      Rules & FAQ

      What counts?

      X reply: your reply under the selected Haunt, posted after it opened. X post: your own original post (not a repost or reply) that mentions HALLOWINU, $HALLOWINU or @HIonchains. X meme: your post with an image, GIF or video about HALLOWINU. TikTok: your public video with HALLOWINU / #hallowinu in the caption. Posts must be less than 48 hours old. Write like a human — copy-paste spam is rejected.

      How much XP?

      X reply: the Haunt's reward · X post: 10 XP · X meme: 15 XP · TikTok: 20 XP (after review). The server decides every reward.

      Why does TikTok need a review?

      TikTok does not let us prove automatically that a TikTok account is yours. HALLOWINU checks that the video exists, matches the link and mentions HALLOWINU — then the team confirms it is your account before the XP is added. Your TikTok shows as "in review" until then.

      Why was I rejected?

      The terminal tells you the exact reason: wrong X account, not under the Haunt, too short, text you already used, text copy-pasted by others, or the post could not be found on X.

      How often can I haunt?

      Up to 3 rewarded haunts per 10 minutes, 2 minutes apart, 20 per day (UTC), one per Haunt.

      What happens during cooldown?

      You can still browse Haunts, the leaderboard and activity. The status shows a live countdown until your next haunt can be claimed.

      Can I use someone else's post?

      No. For X, HALLOWINU compares the author with your connected X account (by permanent X user id). Every post or video can only be submitted once, ever — by its creator.

      What if X verification fails?

      If X is busy, your haunt is queued and checked again automatically. You never lose XP because of an outage — and nothing is paid out before it is verified.

      What is Haunt XP for?

      Haunt XP levels up your HALLOWINU player and ranks you on the leaderboard. It is separate from Arcade Points and is not a claim on the SOL prize pool.

      Is this spam?

      No bots, no copy-paste, no flooding: limits keep the pack natural and within X's rules. Fraud (e.g. deleting replies after claiming) is removed by the team.
