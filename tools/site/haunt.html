      Rules & FAQ

      What counts?

      A real reply from your connected X account, posted under the selected Haunt (or inside its thread) after the Haunt opened. Write like a human: short is fine, copy-paste spam is not.

      Why was I rejected?

      The terminal tells you the exact reason: wrong X account, not under the Haunt, too short, text you already used, text copy-pasted by others, or the post could not be found on X.

      How often can I haunt?

      Up to 3 rewarded haunts per 10 minutes, 2 minutes apart, 20 per day (UTC), one per Haunt.

      What happens during cooldown?

      You can still browse Haunts, the leaderboard and activity. The status shows a live countdown until your next haunt can be claimed.

      Can I use someone else's reply?

      No. HALLOWINU compares the author of the reply with your connected X account (by permanent X user id). Someone else's reply is always rejected.

      What if X verification fails?

      If X is busy, your haunt is queued and checked again automatically. You never lose XP because of an outage — and nothing is paid out before it is verified.

      What is Haunt XP for?

      Haunt XP levels up your HALLOWINU player and ranks you on the Haunt leaderboard. It is separate from Arcade Points and not part of the SOL prize pool.

      Is this spam?

      No bots, no copy-paste, no flooding: limits keep the pack natural and within X's rules. Fraud (e.g. deleting replies after claiming) is removed by the team.
