HALLOWINU is a Halloween-themed memecoin project on Solana, led by a Shiba Inu who traded his usual look for a ghost costume. Cute, a little spooky and always ready for trick or treat — he is the ghost dog of Halloween.

The idea is simple: bring the familiar Inu spirit into spooky season and give the community a world of its own. Haunted forests, pixel ghosts, flying bats and a pack that shows up together. The little scenes and hidden details on this site are part of that world.

The community is where the project comes to life. Telegram is our home for conversations, launch announcements and community raids. X is where we share HALLOWINU artwork, memes and updates. Whether you create a meme, join a raid or bring a friend into the pack, you help shape the season.

$HALLOWINU is preparing for its next chapter on Solana. The contract address, buy link and chart are coming soon — follow the official community for announcements.
